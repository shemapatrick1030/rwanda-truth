⚔️ The A.S.C.R. Response Framework

A proven, repeatable method for countering genocide denial in real time. Used by educators, advocates, and researchers worldwide.

A

Acknowledge

Never dismiss or mock. Acknowledge the question or claim respectfully.

S

State Facts

Present verified, documented evidence. Be precise and specific.

C

Cite Sources

Name your source: ICTR, UN, academic research, survivor testimony.

R

Redirect

Guide the conversation toward accountability, memory, and education.
