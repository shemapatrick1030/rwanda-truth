Our Mission

The Rwanda Truth Platform exists to counter genocide denial and distortion through verified evidence, survivor testimonies, and historical documentation about the 1994 Genocide against the Tutsi in Rwanda.

We provide educators, students, researchers, and the public with the tools to understand, recognize, and respond to denialist narratives with factual, evidence-based information.

Our work is grounded in respect for victims and survivors, commitment to historical accuracy, and dedication to education as a tool for prevention.
